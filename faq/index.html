Before you purchase

Common questions

What kind of assets are available?

The catalog includes Japanese-inspired props, RPG and UI icons, and bundles. Contents, dimensions, and item counts vary by product, so please check the individual product page.

Are the PNG files transparent?

The current catalog is made up of transparent PNG asset sets. Please check the individual product page for the exact contents and specifications.

Can I use the assets in commercial projects?

The currently selected catalog products permit commercial use in completed projects. Please check the product-specific license included with each download for the exact terms.

Can I edit, resize, recolor, crop, or composite the assets?

The currently selected catalog products permit these kinds of edits for use in your own projects. Please check the product-specific license included with each download for the exact terms.

Can I redistribute or resell the original asset files?

No. The original asset files may not be redistributed, resold, or repackaged as a standalone asset pack. Please check the product-specific license included with each download for the exact terms.

What is the difference between buying on BOOTH and itch.io?

Both stores provide KojochoStudio products. Choose the storefront and currency that are more convenient for you. Availability and pricing should be checked on the individual product page.

Do bundles contain assets from separately sold packs?

Some bundles include assets from separately sold packs. Check the individual product page for the included contents before purchasing.

Are the assets AI-assisted?

The catalog does not present a single, catalog-wide AI-assisted status. Check the product-specific information included with each download when that detail matters to your project.

Where can I find the exact license terms?

Please check the product-specific license included with each download. The license is the source of truth for that product.

What should I check before purchasing?

Review the product page for its contents, storefront availability, and price, then read the included license for the use conditions that apply to that product.

This guide summarizes common questions and does not replace the product-specific license included with a download.
